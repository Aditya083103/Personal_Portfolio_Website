Baggage AI Claims

An AI assistant that lets air passengers file baggage-damage claims through a chat experience in under two minutes. Scan a QR code at the carousel, upload a photo of the damage and bag tag, and get an instant outcome: an auto-approved compensation voucher, or a reference number for staff review. Powered by a five-agent LangGraph pipeline.

01Overview

The problem, and the approach

Damaged-baggage claims at airports are slow: passengers queue at a counter, fill out forms, and wait days for a resolution. It's a poor last impression, and it burns staff time on claims that are often low-value and could be settled automatically.

This solution proved an agent pipeline could triage claims in real time, auto-approving simple, low-risk cases and routing only genuinely ambiguous, high-value, or suspicious ones to a human. It was scoped and delivered to the airline client as a focused engagement, with every AI capability behind a swappable interface so the stack can be upgraded without touching business logic.

03Engineering

Engineering highlights

Single-responsibility agents

Five LangGraph agents (conversation, vision, OCR, decision, notification) each own one job and hand a shared claim state forward. Clean, testable, and easy to reason about under real-image edge cases.

Vision + OCR evidence

Damage classification and luxury-bag detection from photos, plus bag-tag OCR for flight number, PNR, and bag ID, with confidence-gated retries that ask for a clearer photo instead of guessing.

Fraud detection & routing

Perceptual image hashing (pHash) catches duplicate-photo submissions, and claim-frequency checks flag repeat filers. A computed fraud score drives the auto vs. staff routing decision, with all thresholds configurable.

Provider-agnostic architecture

LLM, vision, and OCR each sit behind an abstract interface, so swapping a provider is a single environment-variable change. Every call is wrapped so the pipeline degrades gracefully rather than crashing.

Human-in-the-loop dashboard

A staff review dashboard surfaces the escalated claims with full evidence (photo, extracted tag data, and fraud signals), so a reviewer can act on a complete picture in seconds.

Shipped clean in three weeks

Delivered on a disciplined git workflow with a full automated test suite (unit, integration, and smoke tests), passing with zero failures at the v1.0 release gate.

04Impact

What it changed at the counter

A counter queue replaced with a QR code

Passengers go from waiting in line and filling paper forms to a two-minute self-serve claim, with straightforward cases auto-approved on the spot instead of days later.

Staff time freed for the cases that need it

Fraud checks and low-confidence claims route to a human-in-the-loop lane automatically, so staff only review what actually needs judgment. The rest resolves itself.
